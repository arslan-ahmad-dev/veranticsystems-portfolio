Overview

What it is

A marketplace where many vendors sell through one storefront. Vendors manage products and orders, customers check out once across vendors, and admins control commissions and payouts.

The problem

Why it was needed

Selling through generic platforms meant no control over commissions or vendor onboarding, and multi-seller carts and split payouts were not supported.

1 cartmany vendors, one checkout
Splitpayments divided and commissions applied
Fastsearch and filters on large catalogues

Features

What We Built

Vendor onboarding

Applications, approval and per-vendor settings.

Catalogue

Products, variants, stock and media per vendor.

Multi-vendor cart

One checkout that splits into vendor orders.

Split payments

Stripe Connect payouts with commission rules.

Order management

Per-vendor fulfilment and shipment status.

Search

Elasticsearch-powered search and filters.
