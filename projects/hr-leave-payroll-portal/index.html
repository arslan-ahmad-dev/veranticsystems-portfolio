Overview

What it is

An HR portal covering employee records, leave and attendance, shift rosters, payroll runs and payslips, with configurable approval chains.

The problem

Why it was needed

HR ran on spreadsheets and email threads. Leave balances were wrong, payroll took days to calculate and approvals got lost.

Approvalsconfigurable chains for leave and expenses
Auditedpayroll runs logged and re-runnable
