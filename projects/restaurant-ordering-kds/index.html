Restaurant Ordering & Kitchen Display System

Customers order from a QR code at the table or online. Orders appear instantly on kitchen screens, and the front of house sees status and takes payment.

Overview

What it is

A QR-code ordering system with a kitchen display. Guests scan, order and pay at the table or online; tickets show up instantly on the right kitchen station; staff track status from a tablet.

The problem

Why it was needed

Paper tickets and shouted orders caused mistakes and slow tables, and third-party ordering apps took a large cut and owned the customer relationship.

Instantorders reach the right station in real time
Table or onlineone menu, two ways to order
Multi-sitereports per location

Features

What We Built

QR table ordering

Guests order and pay from their phone, no app to install.

Kitchen display

Tickets by station with timers, bump buttons and recall.

Menu management

Categories, modifiers, allergens and live availability.

Payments

Card payments and split bills through Stripe.

Staff tablet

Table status, call-waiter alerts and order edits.

Multi-location

Owner view across sites with sales and prep-time reports.
