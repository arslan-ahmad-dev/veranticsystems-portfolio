Overview

What it is

A booking platform that many businesses use as their own. Each tenant gets a branded booking site, staff calendars, services and pricing, deposits and recurring membership plans, while the team maintains a single codebase.

The problem

Why it was needed

Small service businesses juggle calendars, payment links and spreadsheets, and off-the-shelf tools either look generic or charge per seat. The goal was a branded, subscription-ready booking product that onboards a new business in minutes.

1 codebaseserves every tenant with its own branding
Livecalendar changes appear instantly for everyone
Auditedevery payment event is logged and retried safely

Features

What We Built

Tenant branding

Logo, colours, domain and booking rules per business.

Staff calendars

Availability, breaks, services and buffers per team member.

Deposits and plans

One-off deposits and recurring memberships through Stripe.

Live updates

Socket.io keeps calendars in sync across staff and client screens.

Roles and access

Owner, staff and client roles with tenant-scoped data.

Billing events

Webhook handling with idempotency, retries and an audit trail.
