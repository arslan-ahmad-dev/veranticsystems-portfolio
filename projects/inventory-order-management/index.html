Overview

What it is

An inventory and order system for a distributor: multiple warehouses, purchase and sales orders, invoicing and barcode scanning, with automatic reorder suggestions when stock runs low.

The problem

Why it was needed

Stock counts differed between warehouses and spreadsheets, orders were re-keyed between systems, and reordering depended on someone remembering.
