Overview

What it is

A telehealth platform for clinics. Patients choose a clinician and slot, pay online and join a video room from the browser. Clinicians get a calendar, secure notes and follow-up prompts.

The problem

Why it was needed

Clinics were using a mix of phone calls, generic video links and manual invoices. Patients got lost between steps, and notes lived in personal documents.

Browser videono app install for patients
Pay firstpayment tied to each appointment
Privatenotes visible only to the care team

Features

What We Built

Booking

Clinician availability, slots and service types.

Video rooms

WebRTC consultations with a waiting room.

Reminders

Email and SMS reminders reduce no-shows.

Clinical notes

Secure notes per session with role-based access.

Payments

Card payment and invoices per appointment.

Follow-ups

Prompts and re-booking after each consultation.
