Overview

What it is

A dispatch console for delivery and field-service fleets. One live map shows vehicles, jobs and ETAs; drivers update status from a lightweight mobile web view; customers follow their delivery from a tracking link.

The problem

Why it was needed

Dispatch ran on phone calls and spreadsheets. Nobody could see where vehicles were, customers kept calling for ETAs, and proof of delivery was scattered across chat messages.

1 mapall vehicles, jobs and ETAs live
Tracking linkscustomers stop calling for updates
PODproof of delivery stored with each job

Features

What We Built

Live map

Vehicle positions and job states update in real time.

Job assignment

Drag a job onto a driver, with route and ETA suggestions.

Driver web view

Large status buttons, navigation hand-off and photo capture.

Customer tracking

A shareable link with live status and ETA.

Proof of delivery

Photo, signature and timestamp stored with the job.

History and exports

Filter by driver, date or status and export to CSV.

Architecture

How It Fits Together

  2. Node / Express APIjobs, drivers, auth
  3. Event streamSocket.io, batched writes
  4. MongoDBjobs, events, POD
  5. Dispatcher consoleReact + Mapbox

Location pings are batched before they reach the database, so the live view stays fast as the fleet grows.
